GoodSleep Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-13

Summary

GoodSleep does not collect any personal data. It has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app reads stays on your iPhone.

What the app reads

With your permission, GoodSleep reads the following from Apple Health (HealthKit): sleep analysis, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), respiratory rate, blood oxygen and sleeping wrist temperature. The last three are only used to show how complete your data is. GoodSleep never writes anything to Apple Health.

You can also enter a night's bedtime and wake time by hand. Those entries stay inside GoodSleep.

Where the data is kept

The data is stored in a local database inside the app on your device. A short summary of last night is stored in the device Keychain so the home screen widget can show it.

Like other app data, this may be included in your iCloud or computer backups, according to your own backup settings. GoodSleep itself never sends it anywhere.

Sharing

GoodSleep does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone. The app makes no network connections and contains no third-party SDKs. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

Your choices

You can turn off GoodSleep's access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › GoodSleep. Deleting the app removes all data it has stored on your device. Hand-entered nights can be edited or deleted in the Sources tab.

Medical disclaimer

GoodSleep is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you have concerns about your sleep or health, talk to a healthcare professional.

Children

GoodSleep is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: xingfu.3@gmail.com
